# AI-Generated Content Policy

**Effective date:** on ratification. **Applies to:** Birthright Foundation
staff, contractors, facilitators, artist partners, vendor partners, and any
member of the public whose contribution is published on `birthright.live`.
Contact: `ai-policy@birthright.live`.

This policy governs how Birthright uses generative artificial intelligence
(collectively, "AI Tools") in producing content that appears on the site,
in workshop materials, in email, and in downstream partner communications.
It is designed to align with the EU AI Act (Reg. 2024/1689) transparency
regime, US FTC guidance on AI-generated endorsements, and the reasonable
expectations of members that human authorship is not being misrepresented.

## 1. Definitions

- **AI Tool.** Any large-language-model, diffusion-based image model,
  text-to-audio, text-to-video, or generative-code tool operated by
  Birthright or by a Birthright sub-processor (Anthropic Claude, OpenAI,
  Google Gemini, ElevenLabs, and successors).
- **AI-Assisted.** Human-authored content edited, expanded, translated,
  summarised, or fact-checked by an AI Tool where a human retains
  editorial control.
- **AI-Generated.** Content produced primarily by an AI Tool with limited
  human editing after generation. Includes storefront product imagery,
  vision captions, help-assistant responses, and marketing copy drafts.
- **Human-Authored.** Content authored by a person without material AI
  assistance, or where AI use was limited to spell-check or grammar
  suggestions.

## 2. Disclosure

- **On-page label.** Every published surface that is materially
  AI-Generated carries a visible label (footer, image caption, or byline)
  identifying it as such. AI-Assisted content is not labelled unless a
  reader would be misled without it.
- **Help Assistant.** Every response from the Birthright Help Assistant
  is presented under an "AI · Help Assistant" heading with a link to this
  policy.
- **Facilitator persona bios.** AI-generated portrait imagery of
  Birthright facilitators or the Executive Director is labelled
  "AI portrait" in the alt text and in the visible caption.
- **Community posts.** Members who post materially AI-Generated content
  in community threads must disclose that in the post; falsification is a
  violation of the Community Standards Policy.

## 3. Prohibited Uses

Birthright and its partners shall not use AI Tools to:

- Impersonate a real person, past or present, in a way that could
  reasonably be mistaken for that person.
- Fabricate testimonials, endorsements, reviews, or first-person stories
  attributed to a real or invented member.
- Generate content depicting minors in any private, medical, or intimate
  context.
- Produce clinical or therapeutic advice that could be mistaken for
  guidance from a licensed practitioner.
- Generate content that infringes third-party copyright, trademark, or
  personality rights.

## 4. Data Handling & Training

- **Member data in prompts.** Birthright does not include personally
  identifiable member data in prompts to third-party AI Tools unless the
  member has explicitly consented and a sub-processor DPA is in place.
- **Opt-out of training.** Where a sub-processor offers an opt-out from
  using Birthright prompts for future model training, Birthright takes
  that opt-out by default.
- **Prompt retention.** Prompts and responses are retained in an audit log
  for troubleshooting and safety review for a maximum of ninety (90) days,
  after which they are deleted.

## 5. Human Review

- **Public surfaces.** Every AI-Generated public surface is reviewed by a
  human before publication.
- **Help Assistant safety layer.** The Help Assistant is configured with
  a safety-topic list (self-harm, medical emergency, legal advice, child
  safeguarding). When a member's prompt matches a listed topic, the
  assistant refers the member to a human or to a linked resource instead
  of answering with a generative response.
- **Escalation.** Any AI-Generated response that a member flags via the
  "Report this response" control is reviewed by a Birthright reviewer
  within two (2) business days.

## 6. Rights & Ownership

- **AI-Generated output.** Copyright status of AI-generated output is
  jurisdiction-dependent; Birthright does not claim exclusive copyright
  in output produced solely by an AI Tool. Birthright does claim
  copyright in the human-authored selection, arrangement, and captioning
  of that output where those steps meet the originality threshold.
- **Third-party AI Tool TOS.** All AI use is subject to the operating
  terms of the underlying tool (Emergent LLM Key umbrella TOS, and any
  successor terms). Nothing in this policy overrides a sub-processor's
  own use restrictions.

## 7. Changes

Birthright may update this policy from time to time; material updates
will be signalled with a version bump and a `/legal/history` entry.

## 8. Contact

Questions and concerns: `ai-policy@birthright.live`. Public reports of
policy violations may also be filed through the Ombudsman at
`ombudsman@birthright.live`.

## EU / UK Compliance Addendum

This document was drafted with EU / UK regulatory alignment in mind. Where
this document conflicts with mandatory rights of an EU or UK resident, the
mandatory local rules prevail. In particular:

- **GDPR (EU) 2016/679 and UK GDPR** apply to processing of personal data
  of individuals in the EEA and UK. Where Birthright is the controller,
  the lawful bases relied on are: (i) contract necessity, (ii) legitimate
  interests (balanced by opt-out), (iii) consent (marketing and non-
  essential cookies), and (iv) legal obligation.
- **ePrivacy Directive (2002/58/EC as amended)** applies to cookies and
  similar tracking. Non-essential cookies require prior opt-in consent.
- **EU Consumer Rights Directive (2011/83/EU)** — EU/UK consumers have
  a 14-day right of withdrawal on distance sales of goods and most
  digital services. Where a workshop or digital product has been fully
  performed with the consumer's prior consent, the right may be lost.
- **Digital Services Act (Regulation 2022/2065)** applies to online
  intermediaries offering services in the EU; Birthright's community
  features (posts, DMs, disputes) fall within scope. A single point of
  contact is designated at eu-contact@birthright.live.
- **EU AI Act (Regulation 2024/1689)** applies to providers and deployers
  of AI systems affecting EU users. Birthright's AI features (Help
  Assistant, image generation, image captioning) are labeled as AI-
  assisted and are not used for automated decisions with legal effect.
- **Data transfers out of the EEA/UK/CH** rely on the EU Standard
  Contractual Clauses (2021/914) and the UK International Data Transfer
  Addendum, with a transfer-impact assessment on file for each sub-
  processor.
- **Data Protection Officer (DPO)** — Birthright will designate a DPO
  once threshold criteria under GDPR Art. 37 are met. Contact:
  dpo@birthright.live.
- **DSAR window** — subject access, rectification, erasure, portability,
  and objection requests are responded to within **30 days** (extendable
  by 60 days for complex requests) at privacy@birthright.live.
- **Supervisory authority** — EU/UK residents may lodge a complaint with
  their local supervisory authority; a list is available at
  https://edpb.europa.eu/about-edpb/about-edpb/members_en (EU) and
  https://ico.org.uk (UK).
- **VAT** — Where Birthright's EU B2C digital-service or goods sales
  cross applicable thresholds, VAT registration (One-Stop-Shop or
  country-by-country) will be completed.
