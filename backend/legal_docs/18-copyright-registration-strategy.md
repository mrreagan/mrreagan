# Copyright Registration Strategy (Draft)

## 1. Assets Worth Registering
- **Workshop curricula** — script, workbook, slide deck. Register as a
  literary work.
- **Research articles** — long-form articles under /research. Register in
  batches per calendar quarter as a compilation.
- **Video recordings** of workshops — register as an audiovisual work.
- **Original photography** — group-register per calendar year.
- **AI-generated storefront imagery** — **do not register**. Under current
  U.S. Copyright Office guidance, AI-generated works generally are not
  copyrightable absent significant human authorship. Document the human
  authorship (prompting, curation, layout) if we decide to attempt
  registration on any composite piece.

## 2. Cadence
Quarterly batch filings for articles and photos; per-work filing for
workshop curricula upon completion.

## 3. Notice
Add © Birthright Foundation [year] at the footer of each protected work.

## 4. Assignments
Ensure each contributor (facilitators, contract writers, videographers,
photographers) signs a written IP assignment or work-for-hire clause.
