# Ombudsman Charter (Draft)

## 1. Purpose
The Ombudsman is an independent role responsible for receiving,
investigating, and mediating disputes within the Birthright community.

## 2. Appointment
The Board appoints the Ombudsman for a term of [2 years], renewable.
The Ombudsman may be removed only by a supermajority Board vote.

## 3. Independence
The Ombudsman does not report to any operating officer and communicates
findings directly to the Board.

## 4. Scope
Disputes cognizable by the Ombudsman include: participant-vs-facilitator
concerns, buyer-vs-vendor complaints beyond the Refund & Returns Policy,
allegations of Community Standards violations, and grievances against
Birthright officers or volunteers.

Not in scope: emergencies (which are referred to local emergency
services), clinical or legal complaints (which are referred out), and
matters already in litigation or arbitration.

## 5. Process
1. Intake through `/dashboard/disputes` or by email to ombudsman@birthright.live.
2. Acknowledgement within 3 business days.
3. Preliminary review within 15 business days.
4. Mediation or formal recommendation within 45 business days.
5. Right of appeal to the full Board within 30 days of recommendation.

## 6. Confidentiality
The Ombudsman will keep all identifying information confidential to the
extent possible while conducting the review.

## 7. Anti-Retaliation
No party will be retaliated against for raising a good-faith concern
through the Ombudsman.

## 8. Records
The Ombudsman maintains a summary record of each matter, retained for
7 years.

## 9. Reporting
The Ombudsman provides an anonymized quarterly report to the Board.
