# Content Moderation & Community Standards Policy (Draft)

## 1. Our Ask
Birthright is a community focused on secure connection. Please participate
with courage, honesty, and care. This policy explains what is not
acceptable and how we enforce it.

## 2. Not Allowed
- Harassment, targeted abuse, hate speech
- Threats of violence or incitement
- Doxxing or sharing another person's private information
- Sexual content involving minors (mandatory report to authorities)
- Illegal content or activity
- Spam, promotions, or unsolicited commercial messages
- Impersonation of Birthright staff or another member
- Circumventing moderation (e.g., ban evasion)

## 3. Sensitive Topics
Discussions of trauma, mental health, and relational harm are welcome and
often central to the community. Please add content warnings for intense
material. If you are describing self-harm intent, our team is required to
respond and connect you to appropriate resources.

## 4. Enforcement
- Warning
- Content removal
- Temporary suspension
- Permanent ban
- Referral to law enforcement in cases of imminent harm or illegal
  content

## 5. Reporting
Report content via the report link on any post, or by emailing
ombudsman@birthright.live.

## 6. Appeals
Sanctioned users may appeal to the Ombudsman under the Ombudsman Charter.

## 7. Transparency
We publish an anonymized annual report of the number of actions taken by
category.
