# Facilitator Curriculum Licence Rider

**Effective date:** on countersignature. **Rider to:** the Facilitator
Services Agreement between Birthright Foundation ("Birthright") and the
undersigned facilitator ("Facilitator"). Contact: `facilitators@birthright.live`.

This rider governs the licence and use of Birthright-authored workshop
curricula, facilitator guides, participant workbooks, slides, and
audio-visual materials (collectively, the "Curriculum") when delivered by
the Facilitator inside or alongside Birthright programming.

## 1. Licence Grant

Subject to the Facilitator's compliance with this rider and the base
Facilitator Services Agreement, Birthright grants the Facilitator a
non-exclusive, non-transferable, royalty-free, worldwide licence to use
the Curriculum solely for the purpose of delivering workshops on
`birthright.live` or at Birthright-approved partner venues.

## 2. Restrictions

Facilitator shall not:

- Sub-licence, resell, or redistribute the Curriculum in whole or in part.
- Publish the Curriculum on the open internet, on paid course platforms,
  or on any surface not operated by Birthright.
- Modify the Curriculum in a way that alters the underlying model or
  removes Birthright attribution. Delivery tone, examples, and framing may
  be personalised; the core content, exercises, and citations must remain
  intact.
- Use the Curriculum, or content substantially derived from it, in
  competing programs run outside Birthright.

## 3. Attribution

Every delivery of the Curriculum includes the credit line:
_"Adapted from Birthright Foundation curriculum, used under licence."_
on the slide deck, workbook cover, or verbal introduction, as appropriate
to the format.

## 4. Ownership

Birthright retains all right, title, and interest in the Curriculum,
including all copyrights, trademarks, trade secrets, and moral rights.
Nothing in this rider transfers ownership of the Curriculum to the
Facilitator.

## 5. Contributions Back

- Where the Facilitator authors improvements, extension modules, new
  exercises, or facilitator notes on the Curriculum during the engagement,
  those improvements are commissioned as works made for hire under 17
  U.S.C. § 101; to the extent any element is not a work made for hire, the
  Facilitator irrevocably assigns to Birthright all right, title, and
  interest in that element.
- **Attribution back.** Contributing facilitators receive a "contributions
  from" credit in the next revision of the Curriculum, unless they ask in
  writing to be uncredited.
- **Background IP.** Frameworks, methods, or materials the Facilitator
  brings to the engagement that pre-date the licence remain the
  Facilitator's own property.

## 6. Term & Termination

- **Term.** The licence is co-terminous with the base Facilitator Services
  Agreement.
- **Termination.** On termination or non-renewal of the base agreement,
  the licence ends immediately. The Facilitator shall destroy or return
  all Birthright-supplied materials within thirty (30) days and cease all
  further use.
- **Post-termination residual.** The Facilitator may retain a personal-use
  copy for training-history and continuing-education records only, not
  for redelivery.

## 7. Warranties, Indemnity, Governing Law

Follow the underlying base agreement without modification. Section 2
(Restrictions), 4 (Ownership), and 5 (Contributions Back) survive
termination indefinitely.

## 8. Signatures

Executed by the Facilitator and by an authorised officer of Birthright
Foundation on the effective date shown above.

## EU / UK Compliance Addendum

This document was drafted with EU / UK regulatory alignment in mind. Where
this document conflicts with mandatory rights of an EU or UK resident, the
mandatory local rules prevail. In particular:

- **GDPR (EU) 2016/679 and UK GDPR** apply to processing of personal data
  of individuals in the EEA and UK. Where Birthright is the controller,
  the lawful bases relied on are: (i) contract necessity, (ii) legitimate
  interests (balanced by opt-out), (iii) consent (marketing and non-
  essential cookies), and (iv) legal obligation.
- **ePrivacy Directive (2002/58/EC as amended)** applies to cookies and
  similar tracking. Non-essential cookies require prior opt-in consent.
- **EU Consumer Rights Directive (2011/83/EU)** — EU/UK consumers have
  a 14-day right of withdrawal on distance sales of goods and most
  digital services. Where a workshop or digital product has been fully
  performed with the consumer's prior consent, the right may be lost.
- **Digital Services Act (Regulation 2022/2065)** applies to online
  intermediaries offering services in the EU; Birthright's community
  features (posts, DMs, disputes) fall within scope. A single point of
  contact is designated at eu-contact@birthright.live.
- **EU AI Act (Regulation 2024/1689)** applies to providers and deployers
  of AI systems affecting EU users. Birthright's AI features (Help
  Assistant, image generation, image captioning) are labeled as AI-
  assisted and are not used for automated decisions with legal effect.
- **Data transfers out of the EEA/UK/CH** rely on the EU Standard
  Contractual Clauses (2021/914) and the UK International Data Transfer
  Addendum, with a transfer-impact assessment on file for each sub-
  processor.
- **Data Protection Officer (DPO)** — Birthright will designate a DPO
  once threshold criteria under GDPR Art. 37 are met. Contact:
  dpo@birthright.live.
- **DSAR window** — subject access, rectification, erasure, portability,
  and objection requests are responded to within **30 days** (extendable
  by 60 days for complex requests) at privacy@birthright.live.
- **Supervisory authority** — EU/UK residents may lodge a complaint with
  their local supervisory authority; a list is available at
  https://edpb.europa.eu/about-edpb/about-edpb/members_en (EU) and
  https://ico.org.uk (UK).
- **VAT** — Where Birthright's EU B2C digital-service or goods sales
  cross applicable thresholds, VAT registration (One-Stop-Shop or
  country-by-country) will be completed.
