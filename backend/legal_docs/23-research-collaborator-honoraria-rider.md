# Research Collaborator Honoraria Rider

**Effective date:** on countersignature. **Rider to:** the Master Services or
Volunteer Agreement between Birthright Foundation ("Birthright") and the
undersigned collaborator ("Collaborator"). Contact: `research@birthright.live`.

This rider governs one-off or periodic honoraria paid to academic, clinical,
or lived-experience contributors for research work that is published on
`/research`, cited in Birthright programming, or otherwise integrated into
Foundation deliverables.

## 1. Scope

Covers deliverables including but not limited to: draft chapters or sections,
literature reviews, peer commentary, expert-panel participation, transcript
review, illustrative case notes (de-identified), and one-off editorial passes.
Excluded: employment-tenure work, ongoing salaried research (a separate
services agreement applies), and clinical services to Birthright members.

## 2. Honoraria

- **Amount.** Set out in Schedule A, or in the individual engagement letter
  countersigned by both parties.
- **Tax posture.** Honoraria are treated as US taxable income to the
  Collaborator. Birthright issues Form 1099-NEC when annual payments to the
  Collaborator meet or exceed the IRS reporting threshold. Collaborators
  outside the US receive gross payment; local tax reporting is the
  Collaborator's responsibility.
- **Reimbursables.** Reasonable pre-approved expenses (research materials,
  travel, database access) are reimbursed against receipt.

## 3. IP Assignment & Licence

- **Newly authored deliverables** are commissioned as works made for hire
  under 17 U.S.C. § 101; to the extent any element is not a work made for
  hire, the Collaborator irrevocably assigns to Birthright all right, title,
  and interest in that element.
- **Background IP.** Pre-existing tools, corpora, or IP the Collaborator
  brings to the engagement remain the Collaborator's property; Birthright
  receives a worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free licence to use it as
  embedded in the deliverable.
- **Attribution.** Collaborator receives byline credit on
  `birthright.live/research` and in any derivative publication, unless the
  Collaborator asks in writing to be uncredited.
- **Moral rights** are waived to the extent permitted by law solely for the
  purposes of editorial polishing, translation, and format adaptation.

## 4. Confidentiality & Data

- **Confidential information** exchanged for the engagement is used only for
  the engagement and returned or destroyed on completion.
- **Human-subject data.** Any identifiable participant data must be
  de-identified per HIPAA Safe Harbour before delivery; the Collaborator
  warrants prior IRB or equivalent ethics-committee coverage.
- **Interview material.** If the deliverable draws on interviews the
  Collaborator conducted, the Collaborator warrants informed consent from
  each interviewee, including a right-to-withdraw window that expired before
  delivery.

## 5. Publication & Corrections

- Birthright edits for house style and length; substantive edits are shared
  with the Collaborator for review before publication.
- Post-publication corrections requested in good faith are issued within a
  reasonable time, with clear note of the change.

## 6. Warranties & Indemnity

- Collaborator warrants that the deliverable is original, non-infringing,
  and free of undisclosed conflicts of interest.
- Each party's liability is capped at the total honoraria paid or payable
  under this rider, except for a breach of confidentiality, an IP-warranty
  breach, or gross negligence.

## 7. Termination

Either party may terminate on 14 days' written notice. Pro-rata honoraria are
payable for work delivered before termination. Sections 3 (IP), 4
(Confidentiality), 6 (Warranties), and 8 (Governing law) survive termination.

## 8. Governing Law

State of incorporation of Birthright Foundation (to be confirmed post-formation);
disputes resolved per the Master Agreement's disputes clause.

## 9. Signatures

Executed by the Collaborator and by an authorised officer of Birthright
Foundation on the effective date shown above.

## EU / UK Compliance Addendum

This document was drafted with EU / UK regulatory alignment in mind. Where
this document conflicts with mandatory rights of an EU or UK resident, the
mandatory local rules prevail. In particular:

- **GDPR (EU) 2016/679 and UK GDPR** apply to processing of personal data
  of individuals in the EEA and UK. Where Birthright is the controller,
  the lawful bases relied on are: (i) contract necessity, (ii) legitimate
  interests (balanced by opt-out), (iii) consent (marketing and non-
  essential cookies), and (iv) legal obligation.
- **ePrivacy Directive (2002/58/EC as amended)** applies to cookies and
  similar tracking. Non-essential cookies require prior opt-in consent.
- **EU Consumer Rights Directive (2011/83/EU)** — EU/UK consumers have
  a 14-day right of withdrawal on distance sales of goods and most
  digital services. Where a workshop or digital product has been fully
  performed with the consumer's prior consent, the right may be lost.
- **Digital Services Act (Regulation 2022/2065)** applies to online
  intermediaries offering services in the EU; Birthright's community
  features (posts, DMs, disputes) fall within scope. A single point of
  contact is designated at eu-contact@birthright.live.
- **EU AI Act (Regulation 2024/1689)** applies to providers and deployers
  of AI systems affecting EU users. Birthright's AI features (Help
  Assistant, image generation, image captioning) are labeled as AI-
  assisted and are not used for automated decisions with legal effect.
- **Data transfers out of the EEA/UK/CH** rely on the EU Standard
  Contractual Clauses (2021/914) and the UK International Data Transfer
  Addendum, with a transfer-impact assessment on file for each sub-
  processor.
- **Data Protection Officer (DPO)** — Birthright will designate a DPO
  once threshold criteria under GDPR Art. 37 are met. Contact:
  dpo@birthright.live.
- **DSAR window** — subject access, rectification, erasure, portability,
  and objection requests are responded to within **30 days** (extendable
  by 60 days for complex requests) at privacy@birthright.live.
- **Supervisory authority** — EU/UK residents may lodge a complaint with
  their local supervisory authority; a list is available at
  https://edpb.europa.eu/about-edpb/about-edpb/members_en (EU) and
  https://ico.org.uk (UK).
- **VAT** — Where Birthright's EU B2C digital-service or goods sales
  cross applicable thresholds, VAT registration (One-Stop-Shop or
  country-by-country) will be completed.
