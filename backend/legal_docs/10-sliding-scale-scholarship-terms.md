# Sliding-Scale & Scholarship Terms (Draft)

Birthright offers sliding-scale pricing on many workshops so that cost
does not exclude anyone from the community.

## 1. Tiers
Each participating workshop lists three price points:
- **Supported** — below cost. For participants with financial constraint.
- **Sustaining** — actual cost of running the session.
- **Solidarity** — above cost. Helps subsidize Supported seats.

## 2. Trust-Based
Selection is trust-based. We do not verify income. We ask that you choose
in good faith and adjust up or down over time as your circumstances
change.

## 3. Scholarship Seats
A limited number of no-cost scholarship seats are held for each open
workshop. Apply via the "Request scholarship" link on the workshop page.
Approval is at facilitator discretion.

## 4. Refunds
Same as our general Refund & Returns Policy: refunds available up to 48
hours before the session start time.

## 5. No Downgrade Guilt
Choosing a lower tier does not affect the experience you receive.
Facilitators do not know which tier any participant selected.
