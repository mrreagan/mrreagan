# Founding Partner Uplift Rider

**Effective date:** on countersignature. **Rider to:** the underlying
Facilitator Services Agreement, Artist Consignment / Gallery Partner
Agreement, Vendor Supplier / Dropship Agreement, or Community Partner
Referral Agreement between Birthright Foundation ("Birthright") and the
undersigned partner ("Founding Partner"). Contact: `partners@birthright.live`.

Founding Partners are the first cohort of partners who joined Birthright
before public launch. This rider adds a bounded set of economic and
recognition benefits on top of their base partner agreement, in exchange for
performance and public-recognition commitments during the founding period.

## 1. Eligibility

The Founding Partner status is designated in writing by the Executive Director
at the outset of engagement. Once designated, this rider applies to every
transaction under the base agreement until the founding period ends (§6).

## 2. Economic Uplift

For the duration of the founding period, the Founding Partner receives the
following in addition to the base agreement's economics:

- **Referral / royalty uplift.** Base referral commission or artist royalty
  rate is increased by five (5) percentage points, calculated on the same
  net-sale base as the underlying agreement.
- **Featured placement.** One (1) featured placement per calendar quarter on
  the relevant public surface (`/gallery` featured slot, `/gather`
  spotlight, homepage carousel entry, or `/partner-story` article),
  scheduled by Birthright with Founding Partner input.
- **First-look inventory.** For a rolling 30-day window before broader
  partner outreach, the Founding Partner is given first-look consideration
  on new Birthright-originated product, workshop, or licensing opportunities
  that fall in the Founding Partner's category.
- **Fee waivers.** The Founding Partner is not charged onboarding or
  listing fees, and receives free access to Foundation-hosted training
  and community events during the founding period.

## 3. Recognition

- Public recognition on `/founding-partners` (or the equivalent surface),
  including a partner bio, logo, and story panel.
- Recognition in the annual Foundation impact report.
- An engraved token from the Founder Collection, presented at the end of
  the founding period.

## 4. Commitments

- **Minimum activity.** For each 12-month window inside the founding
  period, the Founding Partner shall (a) fulfil at least one significant
  transaction under the base agreement, and (b) participate in at least one
  co-marketing activity (interview, workshop, guest post, or joint event).
- **Feedback participation.** Attendance at up to two (2) 60-minute
  feedback sessions per year at Birthright's request.
- **Non-disparagement.** Neither party will publicly disparage the other
  or the other's community during the founding period. Nothing here limits
  either party's right to raise safeguarding concerns through the
  Ombudsman channel or to comply with a legal request.

## 5. Confidentiality

Uplift terms, commercial numbers, and internal roadmap materials shared for
co-planning are confidential during the founding period and for two (2)
years after termination.

## 6. Founding-Period Duration & Wind-Down

- **Founding period.** Twenty-four (24) months from the effective date,
  unless terminated earlier under §7 or extended by written agreement.
- **Wind-down.** On expiry, the underlying base agreement continues without
  the uplift. Featured placements booked before expiry are honoured on
  their scheduled dates. Recognition on `/founding-partners` persists.
- **Renewal.** Birthright may in its sole discretion offer a second
  founding-period cohort renewal, or a Legacy Partner designation with
  distinct terms.

## 7. Termination

- **For cause.** Either party may terminate on written notice for a
  material breach not cured within thirty (30) days.
- **Without cause.** Either party may terminate this rider (only — the base
  agreement is unaffected) on ninety (90) days' written notice.
- **Effect.** On termination, the uplift benefits stop immediately;
  commitments in §4 lapse; recognition entries added before termination
  remain unless the Founding Partner asks for removal.

## 8. Warranties, Indemnity, Governing Law

Follow the underlying base agreement without modification.

## 9. Signatures

Executed by the Founding Partner and by an authorised officer of Birthright
Foundation on the effective date shown above.

## EU / UK Compliance Addendum

This document was drafted with EU / UK regulatory alignment in mind. Where
this document conflicts with mandatory rights of an EU or UK resident, the
mandatory local rules prevail. In particular:

- **GDPR (EU) 2016/679 and UK GDPR** apply to processing of personal data
  of individuals in the EEA and UK. Where Birthright is the controller,
  the lawful bases relied on are: (i) contract necessity, (ii) legitimate
  interests (balanced by opt-out), (iii) consent (marketing and non-
  essential cookies), and (iv) legal obligation.
- **ePrivacy Directive (2002/58/EC as amended)** applies to cookies and
  similar tracking. Non-essential cookies require prior opt-in consent.
- **EU Consumer Rights Directive (2011/83/EU)** — EU/UK consumers have
  a 14-day right of withdrawal on distance sales of goods and most
  digital services. Where a workshop or digital product has been fully
  performed with the consumer's prior consent, the right may be lost.
- **Digital Services Act (Regulation 2022/2065)** applies to online
  intermediaries offering services in the EU; Birthright's community
  features (posts, DMs, disputes) fall within scope. A single point of
  contact is designated at eu-contact@birthright.live.
- **EU AI Act (Regulation 2024/1689)** applies to providers and deployers
  of AI systems affecting EU users. Birthright's AI features (Help
  Assistant, image generation, image captioning) are labeled as AI-
  assisted and are not used for automated decisions with legal effect.
- **Data transfers out of the EEA/UK/CH** rely on the EU Standard
  Contractual Clauses (2021/914) and the UK International Data Transfer
  Addendum, with a transfer-impact assessment on file for each sub-
  processor.
- **Data Protection Officer (DPO)** — Birthright will designate a DPO
  once threshold criteria under GDPR Art. 37 are met. Contact:
  dpo@birthright.live.
- **DSAR window** — subject access, rectification, erasure, portability,
  and objection requests are responded to within **30 days** (extendable
  by 60 days for complex requests) at privacy@birthright.live.
- **Supervisory authority** — EU/UK residents may lodge a complaint with
  their local supervisory authority; a list is available at
  https://edpb.europa.eu/about-edpb/about-edpb/members_en (EU) and
  https://ico.org.uk (UK).
- **VAT** — Where Birthright's EU B2C digital-service or goods sales
  cross applicable thresholds, VAT registration (One-Stop-Shop or
  country-by-country) will be completed.
